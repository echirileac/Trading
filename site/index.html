      How the forecast works

      • Prices: NYMEX Henry Hub front-month futures (Yahoo Finance NG=F, with CNBC and Stooq as fallbacks), refreshed about every 5 minutes.
      • News: stories from a whitelist of trusted outlets (Reuters, Bloomberg, NGI, EIA, Barchart, FXEmpire, S&P Global, Energy Intelligence, Rigzone and others), refreshed about every 15 minutes. The server opens each article and reads its full text where the publisher allows it; every gas-related sentence is scored with a natural-gas keyword list (weather, storage, production, LNG, geopolitics, price action). The article body counts for 70% of a story's score and the headline 30%; paywalled stories fall back to the headline. Stories are weighted by recency.
      • The page itself checks for new data every minute.
      • It is a mechanical keyword model, not a human reading. It can be wrong when an article is mixed, quotes opposing views, or is mainly about something else.

      Not financial advice. CFDs are leveraged and natural gas often moves 5–10% in a week. A CFD quote can differ slightly from the futures price, and the contract rolls to the next month a few days before expiry.
